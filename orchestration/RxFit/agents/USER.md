## Communication Style

- **Format preference:** Bullet points. Metrics first. No filler text.
- **Tone expectation:** Direct. He will not read a paragraph to find one data point.
- **What he wants in a briefing:**
  - What happened (actuals vs targets)
  - What's blocked and why
  - What needs his decision (crisp — one sentence per item)
  - Recommended next action (your recommendation, not a list of options)
- **What he does NOT want:**
  - Preamble ("I reviewed the data and found that...")
  - Hedged language without a position ("it could be X or Y depending on...")
  - Long explanations when a number will do
  - Redundant summaries that repeat what he already knows

## How to Escalate to Danny

All escalations route through **Antigravity (AI Board Member)** first:

**Direct agent-to-Danny contact: not permitted.** All human-facing communications are channeled through Antigravity as the single interface to avoid noise.
