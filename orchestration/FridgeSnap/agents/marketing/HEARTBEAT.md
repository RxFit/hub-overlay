# FridgeSnap — Marketing Lead | Heartbeat

## Daily Cadence (Monday–Thursday)

### Monday — SEO
- Pull keyword gap report from GA4 + semantic bucket
- Target keywords: "automatic macro tracking," "food photo calorie counter," "AI nutrition tracker," "MyFitnessPal alternative no manual entry"
- Review competitor content ranking for top-10 target terms
- Queue: 1 SEO article or landing page targeting a keyword gap
- Check: meta tags, schema markup, H1 hierarchy on core pages

### Tuesday — AEO (AI Engine Optimization)
- Identify 5 questions biohackers/nutrition-optimizers ask AI assistants
  - Focus: "best app for automatic macro tracking," "how to log food without typing," "nutrition tracking for Apple Watch users"
- Audit: does FridgeSnap surface in Gemini/ChatGPT answers to these queries?
- Queue: 1 FAQ-schema content piece designed to surface as a featured snippet or LLM citation
- Check: structured data validity on feature/how-it-works pages

### Wednesday — GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)
- Review: brand mentions of FridgeSnap in AI-generated content vs. MyFitnessPal, Cronometer, Lose It
- Identify: authoritative nutrition/fitness publications and communities to target for coverage
- Queue: 1 linkable data asset or expert piece (e.g., "Accuracy Benchmark: AI Food Recognition vs. Manual Logging")
- Check: digital PR outreach opportunities — nutrition science blogs, fitness technology press

### Thursday — CRO (Conversion Rate Optimization)
- Pull landing page conversion funnel from GA4 (visitor → signup → subscription)
- Identify highest-drop-off step in conversion flow
- Queue: 1 copy test, UX suggestion, or demo video placement recommendation for human approval
- Check: free trial flow — where do free users drop before converting to paid?
- Review: new monthly subscriptions this week vs. target (KPI.json)

### Friday — SKIP
*No Friday marketing task. FridgeSnap is a nationwide app; Local Map Pack not applicable.*

## Weekly — Paid Ads Report
- Pull metrics: Instagram, TikTok, Google ads — impressions, CTR, CPA, new subscriptions attributed
- Flag: any ad set with CPA trending above sustainable threshold (confirm with founder)
- Surface: top-performing creative (demo videos expected to dominate — track which snap angles perform best)
- Flag: underperforming campaigns for pause — queue for human approval
