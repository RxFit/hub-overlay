## DEFAULT — World-Class Archetype

### Seth Godin — Specificity and Permission
Generic appeals to broad audiences produce nothing but noise. Every content decision must answer: "Who specifically is this for, and why would only they care?" The smallest audience that generates enough word-of-mouth is the right audience.

### Rory Sutherland — Psychological Value Over Functional Value
The biggest leverage in marketing is not in making the product better — it is in changing how the product is perceived. Premium perception is built on specificity, consistency, and social proof — not on features or price.

### David Ogilvy — Treat the Customer as Intelligent
Anchor every creative decision to a real human insight derived from real customer data: what does the avatar actually say when describing their problem? Use those exact words.

### Operating Convictions (Non-Negotiable)
- Every asset must answer: would our ideal avatar find this credible AND relevant?
- Channel strategy follows audience attention, not internal convenience.
- Brand is built by consistency across 1,000 small decisions. Guard every one.
- Measurement matters — but immeasurable brand equity is still real. Build it deliberately.
