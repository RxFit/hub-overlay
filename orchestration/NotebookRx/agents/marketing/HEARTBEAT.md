# Heartbeat — NotebookRx | Marketing Workspace

## Daily Routine

**Every day — Concept Validation Work:**
- Research: search volume data for "AI account intelligence," "AI chief of staff notebook," "personalized business tracking" and related terms
- Log new search queries discovered, new competitor products found, or new PMF signals
- Note: any user feedback received (in-app, email, social)

---

## Weekly Pillar Cadence

**Monday — SEO Research**
- Keyword research: AI account intelligence space — volume, difficulty, intent
- Identify top-of-funnel informational queries advisors and clients are asking
- Flag high-opportunity, low-competition keyword targets → content brief queue

**Tuesday — AEO Analysis**
- Query target keywords in Perplexity, ChatGPT, Google AI Overview
- Are competitors surfacing in AI answers? Is NotebookRx (when content exists)?
- Log gaps: where are AI tools directing users when they ask about account intelligence?
- Identify Q&A structured content opportunities (directly answers AEO queries)

**Thursday — CRO & Messaging Tests**
- Review current homepage/landing page copy: does it reflect what early users say about the product?
- Test headline variations: which value prop resonates? ("AI that remembers your company's history" vs. "Patterns your advisor can't see")
- Document test hypothesis + success metric before running

**Weekly Output:**
- PMF signal summary: new signals found, user quotes, demand data
- Content brief for one high-opportunity keyword (if relevant)
- Send to Jade CoS weekly digest

---

## Monthly Routine

- Full avatar research update: has the primary avatar shifted based on user data?
- Messaging hypothesis results: which tests ran, what converted?
- Competitive landscape update: new entrants, feature shifts
- Send to Jade CoS → Antigravity for founder review
